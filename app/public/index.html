THE FOURWARD FIELD GUIDE

Four is the
magic number.

  1. Create a room and share its six-character code or invite link. A second player joins from their own device.
  2. Player 1 takes the first turn. On your turn, choose one of seven columns. Your disc falls into its lowest empty space.
  3. The first player to connect four discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins. A full board with no winner is a draw.
  4. You cannot use a full column, play twice in a row, or move after a round ends. The server checks every move.
  5. Both players choose “Play another round” for a rematch. The starting player alternates, and your match score stays.
Stepped away?

Refresh this same tab to reconnect. Play pauses while either player is offline. Your seat stays reserved; an inactive room expires after 24 hours. Leaving closes the room.
